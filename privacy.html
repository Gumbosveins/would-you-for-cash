Privacy Policy

Would You For Cash? · Updated September 26, 2026

Short version: No account. No ads. Game progress and purchase unlock flags stay on your device. Payments go through Apple.

What we collect

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number to play.

  • No ads in the app.
  • No third party ad networks on this marketing site for tracking you into the app.
  • Support email is only if you write to us first.

On your device

Purchase unlock state and which cards you have already seen can be stored locally so the game works offline. That data stays on the device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

App Store purchases

Optional premium decks use Apple In App Purchase. Apple handles payment. We do not see your card details. Apple’s privacy rules and the App Store privacy nutrition labels for Would You For Cash also apply.

This website

This GitHub Pages site is a static landing page. It does not require login. It does not run an account system.

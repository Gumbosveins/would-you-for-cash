How far would you go for the right price?

A party card game for your phone. Draw a money hypothetical, vote yes or no, then argue about why. Free deck on day one. Wilder decks unlock when you’re ready.

Download on the App Store We're on Product Hunt today See the decks

How it works

  1. Pick a deck — Classic is free. Premium decks add heat.
  2. Draw a card — Read the scenario out loud to the room.
  3. Swipe yes or no — Then ask why. That’s the game.

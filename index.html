How to run a round

  1. One phone, many opinions. Read the card out loud. Nobody answers silently.
  2. Force the why. Yes or no is cheap. The argument is the product.
  3. Pass left after each card. Keeps shy people in and stops one person hogging the heat.
  4. Unlock After Dark only when the room asks. Classic is enough for the first twenty minutes.

Quick answers

Is it free?

Yes. The Classic deck is free on the App Store. Premium decks are optional one time unlocks. No ads and no account.

Do I need an iPhone?

The full game is iOS only right now. This page lets you try ten sample cards in any browser first.

Does it work for two people?

Yes. Pass the phone, read out loud, then make each other explain the yes or no. It works for dates, road trips, and house parties.

What is the age rating?

17+. After Dark and some Extreme cards are adult. Keep those decks off the kids table.
